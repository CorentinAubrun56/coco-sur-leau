A legendary race held only every two years, celebrating its 50th anniversary with a return to La Rochelle. The start is on **12 September 2027**, with a stopover in **Las Palmas de Gran Canaria** in the Canary Islands, then on to **Salvador de Bahia** in Brazil.

To earn a place on the start line, you first have to qualify: a whole series of races to complete from March 2027. Follow my progress on the [Ranking]({{ '/en/ranking/' | relative_url }}) page.
