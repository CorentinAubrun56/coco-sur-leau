## Leg 1: La Rochelle → Las Palmas de Gran Canaria 🇫🇷 → 🇮🇨

Start from La Rochelle, across the Bay of Biscay, then down the Spanish and Portuguese coasts to the Canary Islands. A stopover in Las Palmas to repair, sleep and get ready for what comes next.

## Leg 2: Las Palmas → Salvador de Bahia 🇮🇨 → 🇧🇷

The big Atlantic crossing, the equator and the Doldrums, all the way to Salvador de Bahia in Brazil. Solo, with no way of communicating with land.

All the details (race sheet, rules, live tracking on race day) are on the [race page]({{ '/en/races/mini-transat-2027/' | relative_url }}).
