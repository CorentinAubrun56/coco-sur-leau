It's official: we just bought a **Mini 6.50**!! 🎉 For those who don't know, it's an offshore racing boat only 6.5 metres long… built to cross oceans!

Meet **Big Bounce, number 1019** (and her mast)! 🔥

{% include gallery class="empile" caption="Big Bounce on her trailer, and her mast waiting in the mast park. · Photos: Corentin Aubrun" %}

## 🎯 The goal: qualify for the Mini Transat!

A solo Atlantic crossing with no way of communicating with land! 📞🚫 A legendary race held only every two years, and the next edition starts on **12 September 2027** (mark your calendars! 📅).

The course is a dream: starting from **La Rochelle** 🇫🇷, a stopover in **Las Palmas de Gran Canaria** in the Canary Islands 🇮🇨, then heading for **Salvador de Bahia**, Brazil! 🇧🇷 ([see the course]({{ '/en/races/mini-transat-2027/' | relative_url }}))

But first, I have to earn my spot! From next March, I need to complete a whole series of qualifying races. Between the weather and breakages, it's going to be tough… but we're giving it everything! 🔧⚡

I'll share the whole adventure here, especially the struggles! Stay tuned! 👀🔥
